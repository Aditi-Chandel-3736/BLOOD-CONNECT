How BloodConnect works

Four simple steps to connect people who need help with potential donors.

01

Register as a donor

Create a profile with your blood group and location so matching requests can find you.

02

Submit a request

Provide the requested blood group, hospital, city, and contact details.

03

Notify potential donors

The system identifies matching available donors and attempts to send email alerts.

04

Confirm the outcome

Donors can respond to an alert and report the outcome of their hospital visit.
